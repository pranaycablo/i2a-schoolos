# 📊 i2A SchoolOS — Append-Only Financial Ledger Specification
> **Double-Entry Ledger & Reversal Tracking Architecture**

---

## 📌 Ledger Principles

- **Append-Only Entries**: Historical transactions are never mutated. Adjustments and refunds post new reversal/credit entries.
- **Entry Types**: `INVOICE`, `PAYMENT`, `DISCOUNT`, `FINE`, `REFUND`, `EXPENSE`.
