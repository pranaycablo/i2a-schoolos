# 📚 i2A SchoolOS — Subject & Curriculum Master Model
> **Subject Types, Mark Breakups & Class Configurations**

---

## 📌 Model Specification

- **Subject Entity**: `id`, `tenantId`, `sessionId`, `classId`, `name`, `code`, `type` (`CORE`, `ELECTIVE`, `OPTIONAL`, `LANGUAGE`, `PRACTICAL`).
- **Mark Breakup Structure**: `theoryMarks`, `practicalMarks`, `internalMarks`, `totalMarks`, `passingMarks`.
- **Subject Groups**: Languages, Science, Mathematics, Social Studies, IT, Co-Curriculars.
