# 🧠 i2A SchoolOS — AI Academic & Decision Assist Architecture
> **Controlled AI Provider Gateway & Quality Control Pipeline**

---

## 📌 1. AI Safety & Controlled Approval Gateway

AI never modifies or publishes critical academic data directly.

```
AI Model Proposal ➔ Format & Schema Validation ➔ Teacher Review Desk ➔ Principal Lock ➔ Database Write
```

---

## 📌 2. AI Subsystem Capabilities

1. **Book OCR & Concept Decomposer**: Upload Book PDF -> Text Extraction -> Chapter & Topic Taxonomy -> Important Concept Extraction.
2. **AI Question Bank Generator**: Generates MCQs, Fill-in-Blanks, Short/Long Answers, and Numericals with difficulty tags.
3. **One-Click AI Test Generator**: Balances total marks, question types, and syllabus coverage automatically.
4. **AI Student Concept Diagnostic**: Identifies chapter-level concept gaps for students and class-wide performance trends.
5. **AI Principal Conversational Assistant**: Natural language querying in Hindi and English ("इस महीने fee collection कितना है?").
